About Us

Built on craftsmanship and trust

West Star Home LTD is a Saskatchewan-based home builder and renovation specialist committed to creating spaces that last. We were founded on a straightforward promise — do quality work, honour every commitment, and keep improving.

Our team brings together experienced tradespeople, project managers, and designers who take pride in craftsmanship and clear communication. Whether we're framing a new home, remodelling a decades-old kitchen, or finishing a basement suite for rental income, we treat every project as if we were building for our own family.

That means honest pricing, honest timelines, and honest conversations — even when they're hard. It's also why so much of our business comes from referrals: homeowners who've worked with us once, and their friends and neighbours who've seen the results.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision

To become Saskatchewan's most trusted name in residential construction — the builder homeowners recommend without hesitation.

We believe a home is more than a structure. It's where families grow, memories form, and long-term financial security is built. Every project we take on carries that weight, and that's why we're relentless about doing things right the first time.

Looking ahead, we're investing in three areas that will define the next chapter of our company:

  • Sustainable building — practices and materials that reduce long-term operating costs and environmental impact for the homeowners we serve.
  • Client transparency — digital tools that give clients real visibility into their project at every stage, from budget to schedule to daily progress.
  • Local craftsmanship — mentoring the next generation of tradespeople so quality home-building stays strong in our community.

We're building for the long run — homes that stand, relationships that last, and a company our community is proud of.
